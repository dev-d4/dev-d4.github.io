01 — About

From raw source data to numbers people trust

I'm an engineer with a Master's degree in Engineering Physics from KTH, working where data engineering, analytics and financial regulation meet.

My work is the full path from raw source systems to analytics-ready data: designing data models, building ETL workflows in Python and SQL on Azure, and making sure what comes out is correct, documented and reliable enough to be used across the business.
